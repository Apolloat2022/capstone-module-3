# Decisions — Module 3 Capstone

> Replace this template with your own reasoning. It is part of the assessment.

## Assumptions

The brief leaves things open on purpose. List what you assumed, and flag which
assumptions you invented rather than inferred.

## Trade-offs

For each significant decision: what you chose, what you rejected, and what the
choice cost you.

## Known limitations

What does not work, what is untested, and what would break first under load or
against messier input.

## With more time

What you would change, in priority order.
